# Explainer workflow (科普短视频)

Short explainers for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts: one
idea, 30–60 seconds, 9:16, English, music only. There is no voice-over,
so the on-screen text is the narration: it has to carry the whole story
for someone watching with the sound off.

## Two tracks: 2D and 3D

Pick the track at the storyboard, from the subject: is it flat (numbers,
lists, comparisons, text) or spatial (a journey through space, objects,
a camera that moves through a world)?

| Track | Folder | Code | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2D | [`2d/`](2d/README.md) | DOM + CSS; CSS 3D transforms for a little depth (cards, drums) | copy → script → storyboard → code → render → video |
| 3D | [`3d/`](3d/README.md) | WebGL with three.js, as a literal voxel diorama: people, places and things the story passes through | copy → script → storyboard → **scene** (literal check) → code → render → video |

Episodes are numbered across both tracks (`ep01` is 2D, `ep02` 3D…),
each a folder `epNN-<slug>/` inside its track, started from that
track's `_template/`.

## Steps (both tracks)

| # | Step | File | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Copy (文案) | `copy.md` | Angle, hook, takeaway and every fact with its source quoted. |
| 2 | Script (脚本) | `script.md` | The exact words on screen, timed in bars. ≤ 12 words up at once, about 3 words a second. |
| 3 | Storyboard (分镜) | `storyboard.md` | One row per shot: what it shows and how it moves; the palette. |
| 3½ | Scene (场景设计), 3D only | `scene.md` | The objects, the camera path as keyframes, lights and materials. |
| 4 | Code (代码) | `index.html`, `music.json` | The page draws every frame from `render(t)`; the music hits the cuts. |
| 5 | Render (渲染/录制) | `out/music.wav`, `out/*.mp4` | Recorded with `engine/video/record.mjs --size portrait`. |
| 6 | Publish (发布) | `publish.md`, `thumb.html` | Watched end to end with sound off. Cover `thumb.html` → `out/thumbnail.png` (`engine/video/thumb.mjs`), text inside the 3:4 crop. Title, description and tags for YouTube Shorts; caption and pinned comment for TikTok; caption for Reels; a first-person post and a sources comment for LinkedIn; cover frame. Every claim and source from `copy.md`, nothing new. |

Don't start the code before the script reads well on its own.

## Rules

- **Every fact has a source** in `copy.md`, quoted, and the end card
  names it. If a number exists only in an image or a paywall, leave it
  out rather than estimate it. AI-written copy gets checked against the
  sources by a person before step 4.
- **Defensive only** for security topics: explain the risk and the fix,
  never the steps of an attack.
- **Illustrations are labelled as such** in `script.md` (example
  passwords, example names): never present them as real data.
- **Safe area.** The platforms draw buttons and captions over the
  video. Keep text inside x 40–620 and y 110–1030 of the 720×1280 CSS
  frame; the bottom ~250 px and the right edge are covered.
- **Palette per episode, fixed structure.** Colour follows the topic;
  what makes an episode recognisably ours is the structure: Archivo and
  JetBrains Mono, square corners and ruled lines, the progress bar along
  the top and "oliverz.dev" top right. Pick the palette in the
  storyboard (step 3) and write down why. Rules for any palette:
  - Mood from the subject: security and the night side of the internet
    read dark; space and physics can be deep blue; biology warm and
    organic; history paper and ink; money green and gold.
  - Colours carry meaning, not decoration: one accent for "the point /
    the good answer", one warning colour used only when something goes
    wrong, and they never swap roles inside an episode. A viewer with
    the sound off should follow the turn of the story by colour alone.
  - At most a ground, a text colour, a muted text colour, one accent and
    one warning colour. Body text at least 7:1 against the ground and
    the accent at least 4.5:1: phones are watched outdoors, small.
  - Avoid pure #000 / #fff fields that flicker in compression, and
    big areas of fully saturated red or blue (banding, harsh on OLED).
  - All colours live as CSS variables in `:root` of `index.html`, so a
    palette change is one block.
- **Timing:** cuts on bar lines. At 100 bpm a bar is 2.4 s; 16 bars is
  38.4 s.
- **Music:** generated by `engine/video/music.mjs` from `music.json`,
  so it is original and free to post. Add a hit on every cut and a
  riser into the twist.

## Language

English first: TikTok, Reels and Shorts reach furthest in English, the
sources are English, and the site embeds the same video on every
locale's page. Other languages are a caption swap, not a re-render: the
caption layer takes `?lang=` (2D pages and 3D `captions.html` alike),
so a Chinese or Portuguese cut reuses the rendered frames and only
re-composites. Chinese cuts would go to Bilibili, which mainland
viewers can reach; YouTube they can't.

On the site, an episode embeds its YouTube Short behind a click-to-load
poster (no third-party request until play); TikTok and Instagram get
links, not embeds.

## Posting

- Caption and hashtags come from `copy.md`.
- Switch on each platform's AI-generated content label: the visuals
  are generated by code and the copy is AI-assisted.
- Platforms may suggest a trending sound instead of the music bed;
  that's a choice per post, the bed stays in the file.
- Videos are never committed (`out/` and any `.mp4` under `explainers/` are gitignored): they live on YouTube, and the site embeds them from there. Keep the finished files locally.
