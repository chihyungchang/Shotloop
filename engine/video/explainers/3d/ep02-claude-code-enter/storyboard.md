# 02 · Storyboard

Step 3. 9:16 (720×1280 CSS, rendered 1080×1920). 3D track, voxel
diorama: the shots here, the world in scene.md. Captions on a dark
panel in the top band.

## Palette

The world keeps natural colours (grass, sea, brick, steel); the story
colours are reserved.

| Role | Colour | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accent: your data | amber `#ffb340` | The prompt and every token. Nothing else in the world is amber, so the eye always finds the payload, from a desk to a seabed. |
| Warning | none | Nothing goes wrong in this story; the permission prompt is white. |
| Sky by place | day blue at home and over the sea, deep blue under water, dark inside the data centre | The journey goes from a lit room, outdoors, under water, into a machine hall. |
| Caption panel | ink `#0b0f19` at 85%, white text | Readable over any sky. |
