# How a fake delivery text message steals your card details, s

## Idea

A fake 'delivery problem' text sends you to a copycat page that asks for a small fee and takes your card details. The video walks through each step on a phone and shows the signs that give it away.

## Hook

Text scams cost Americans $330 million in 2022, and a fake parcel text is one of the most common.

## Copy

A text arrives: your parcel is on hold, pay a small redelivery fee. It comes from an unknown number, it pushes you to hurry, and the link looks odd.

Tap it and a page opens that looks just like a courier's site, but the address in the bar isn't theirs.

It asks for a tiny fee, so you type in your card number, expiry date and security code, then press Pay.

Those details go straight to the scammers, who can use the card themselves.

How to beat it: don't tap. Check your parcel in the courier's own app or website, forward the text to 7726, then delete it.

## Facts

- Reported losses to text message scams in the US reached $330 million in 2022. (source: FTC Consumer Protection Data Spotlight, 'IYKYK: The top text scams of 2022', June 2023 — https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2023/06/iykyk-top-text-scams-2022)
- Fake package delivery problem texts were among the most reported text scams of 2022. (source: FTC Consumer Protection Data Spotlight, 'IYKYK: The top text scams of 2022', June 2023)
- Scam texts often claim a problem with a delivery and include a link; you shouldn't click links in unexpected texts. (source: FTC, 'How To Recognize and Report Spam Text Messages' — https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages)
- You can report spam texts by forwarding them to 7726 (SPAM). (source: FTC, 'How To Recognize and Report Spam Text Messages' — https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages)
- Check a delivery by going to the shipper's official website or app yourself, not through the link in the text. (source: FTC, 'How To Recognize and Report Spam Text Messages')
