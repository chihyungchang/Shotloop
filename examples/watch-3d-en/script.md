# What is inside a mechanical watch, and how does it keep time · Script

1. No battery. What keeps it ticking?
2. Inside: a spring, gears and a beating wheel
3. Winding coils the mainspring: stored energy
4. Gears carry that energy to the hands
5. The escapement releases it tick by tick
6. 8 beats a second, ≈691,200 a day
7. A spring, a wheel, no battery
