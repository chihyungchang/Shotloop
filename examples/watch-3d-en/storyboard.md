# How does a mechanical watch tick? · Storyboard

| # | Time | Shot | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3.5 s | No battery?: The watch floats in a dark studio, three-quarter view, steel case and bright dial, seconds hand sweeping. A warm rim light glints on the case. The caseback turns slightly towards camera, hinting at the gold movement behind a sapphire window. | Slow push-in and orbit of about 20° around the floating watch; seconds hand ticks. |
| 2 | 4.5 s | Exploded view: The watch flies apart into its exploded view along one vertical axis: crystal and dial on top, then hands, gear train, mainspring barrel, escapement and balance wheel, mainplate at the bottom. Small red ruby jewels glow in the plates. Each layer carries a short floating label. | Layers separate one after another with eased motion while the camera rises and tilts down a little. |
| 3 | 4.5 s | The mainspring: Close on the mainspring barrel, its lid cut away: a flat steel spiral spring inside. The crown on the left turns; the spiral coils tighter around the centre arbor, and a warm gold glow builds in the coil as stored energy. | Crown rotates, spring visibly tightens, glow rises; camera dollies in from above at a slight angle. |
| 4 | 4.5 s | The gear train: The barrel's teeth drive a chain of brass gears (centre, third, fourth wheel) leading up to the hands. A gold pulse of light travels from gear to gear as each one turns, ending at the hands on the dial above. | Gears rotate at their ratios; the camera tracks sideways along the chain from barrel to hands. |
| 5 | 5 s | The escapement: Macro on the steel escape wheel and the pallet fork with two red ruby pallets, a small floating label "escapement" beside them. The fork rocks, locks a tooth, releases it, locks the next: the wheel moves in clean steps instead of spinning freely. A small flash marks each tick. | Fork rocks left-right, escape wheel steps one tooth per beat; slow camera push-in, slight orbit. |
| 6 | 5 s | The balance wheel: The gold balance wheel and its blue-steel hairspring, seen from above at an angle. The wheel swings back and forth, the hairspring breathing in and out, nudging the pallet fork at each swing. A counter floating beside it rolls up: 8 per second, then ≈691,200 per day. | Balance oscillates continuously; camera slowly orbits about 30°; counter rolls and lands with a bounce. |
| 7 | 3.5 s | End card: The exploded layers fly back together into the complete watch, which turns face-forward in the dark studio, seconds hand sweeping. | Layers reassemble with eased motion; camera pulls back to the hero framing of shot 1. |
