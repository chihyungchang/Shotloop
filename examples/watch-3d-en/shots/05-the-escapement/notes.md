# 05-the-escapement · The escapement

## Must show

Macro on the steel escape wheel and the pallet fork with two red ruby pallets, a small floating label "escapement" beside them. The fork rocks, locks a tooth, releases it, locks the next: the wheel moves in clean steps instead of spinning freely. A small flash marks each tick.

## On screen

The escapement releases it tick by tick

## Motion

Fork rocks left-right, escape wheel steps one tooth per beat; slow camera push-in, slight orbit.
