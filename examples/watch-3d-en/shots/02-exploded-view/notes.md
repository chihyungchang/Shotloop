# 02-exploded-view · Exploded view

## Must show

The watch flies apart into its exploded view along one vertical axis: crystal and dial on top, then hands, gear train, mainspring barrel, escapement and balance wheel, mainplate at the bottom. Small red ruby jewels glow in the plates. Each layer carries a short floating label.

## On screen

Inside: a spring, gears and a beating wheel

## Motion

Layers separate one after another with eased motion while the camera rises and tilts down a little.
