# 06-the-balance-wheel · The balance wheel

## Must show

The gold balance wheel and its blue-steel hairspring, seen from above at an angle. The wheel swings back and forth, the hairspring breathing in and out, nudging the pallet fork at each swing. A counter floating beside it rolls up: 8 per second, then ≈691,200 per day.

## On screen

8 beats a second, ≈691,200 a day

## Motion

Balance oscillates continuously; camera slowly orbits about 30°; counter rolls and lands with a bounce.
