# 04-the-gear-train · The gear train

## Must show

The barrel's teeth drive a chain of brass gears (centre, third, fourth wheel) leading up to the hands. A gold pulse of light travels from gear to gear as each one turns, ending at the hands on the dial above.

## On screen

Gears carry that energy to the hands

## Motion

Gears rotate at their ratios; the camera tracks sideways along the chain from barrel to hands.
