# Why is the sky blue, and why are sunsets red · Script

1. Same Sun, same air. So why is the sky blue at noon, and red at sunset?
2. Sunlight looks white, but every colour is in it, from short blue waves to long red ones.
3. Each tiny molecule of air scatters that light. Blue is scattered about 5 times more than red.
4. That scattered blue comes at you from every direction, so the whole sky glows blue.
5. At sunset the Sun is low, and its light crosses about 38 times more air to reach you.
6. Along that long path, even more of the blue is scattered away. What reaches you is orange and red.
7. Same air, different path: blue by day, red at dusk.
