# Why is the sky blue, and why are sunsets red · Script

1. Same Sun. Blue sky, red sunset — why?
2. Sunlight carries every colour
3. Air scatters blue ≈6× more than red
4. Blue arrives from every direction: the sky
5. At sunset, light crosses ≈38× more air
6. Blue scatters away; red gets through
7. Same air, different path
