# 04-blue-from-everywhere · Blue from everywhere

## Must show

The camera pulls back and tilts down to a small figure standing on the hill under the noon Sun. From every part of the sky, the scattered blue streaks from the molecule field rain toward the figure's eye from all directions, and as they arrive the empty dark sky fills with glowing blue, patch by patch. The direct beam from the Sun stays white-yellow.

## On screen

Blue arrives from every direction: the sky

## Motion

Camera pull-back and tilt; blue streaks converge on the viewer from all angles; sky fills blue in soft waves until the whole upper frame glows.
