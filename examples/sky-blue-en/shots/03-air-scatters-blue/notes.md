# 03-air-scatters-blue · Air scatters blue

## Must show

The colour strands of the sunlight beam enter the atmosphere: a field of tiny glowing air molecules (recurring, small white-teal dots labelled 'air molecule' once). Each time a blue photon meets a molecule it ricochets off in a random direction as a short blue streak; red photons sail straight through. A big glowing counter at the top rolls 1 → ≈5× with a bounce, beside a small blue dot and red dot.

## On screen

Air scatters blue ≈5× more than red

## Motion

Photons stream in from the left; blue streaks bounce and multiply into a spray, red continues in a clean line; the ≈5× counter rolls up and lands; the camera pushes slowly into the molecule field.
