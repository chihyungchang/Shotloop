# 06-red-gets-through · Red gets through

## Must show

Zoom along the long sunset path of the Earth cross-section: the colour strands of the sunlight beam run through the air molecules. One by one the blue, then green strands peel off sideways and fade out (draining stock), leaving empty gaps in the beam; only orange and red reach the figure. As they arrive the sky behind the figure blooms orange-red and the Sun turns a deep red disc on the horizon.

## Voice-over

Along that long path, even more of the blue is scattered away. What reaches you is orange and red.

## On screen

Blue scatters away; red gets through

## Motion

Strands peel off in sequence from the top, the beam thinning and warming in colour; the remaining red strands reach the eye with a soft flash; sky gradient warms to sunset.
