# 01-blue-sky-red-sunset · Blue sky, red sunset

## Must show

A wide horizon seen from a low hill: a glowing Sun high in a deep blue sky, soft cloud wisps. As the shot runs the Sun sinks toward the horizon and the whole sky floods from blue to orange-red, the Sun swelling and reddening with a lens streak. Silhouetted hill and a lone tree in the foreground give depth; dust motes drift in the light.
