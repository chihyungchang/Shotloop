# How long does sunlight take to reach Earth? · Script

1. You have never seen the Sun as it is right now. Its light takes time to get here.
2. On average, the Sun is about 149.6 million kilometers away. Astronomers call that one astronomical unit.
3. Light in a vacuum covers about 299,792 kilometers every second. That is about 7.5 laps around the equator.
4. Divide the distance by the speed, and you get about 499 seconds. That is 8 minutes and 19 seconds.
5. Light from the Moon takes about 1.3 seconds. From the Sun to Neptune, it takes about 4 hours and 10 minutes.
6. So when sunlight warms your face, you're feeling light that set out about 8 minutes ago.
7. Light is quick, but the universe is so much bigger.
