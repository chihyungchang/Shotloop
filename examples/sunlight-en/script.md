# How long does sunlight take to reach Earth? · Script

1. How long does sunlight take to reach Earth? The Sun you see right now is the Sun as it was.
2. The mean distance from the Sun to Earth: 149.6 million km = 1 astronomical unit.
3. In a vacuum, light covers 299,792 km every second, about 7.5 times around Earth's equator.
4. 149.6 million km ÷ 299,792 km/s ≈ 499 s: about 8 min 19 s.
5. Compare: Moon → Earth 1.3 s; Sun → Earth 8 min 19 s; Sun → Neptune about 4 h 10 min.
6. The sunlight on your face left the Sun 8 minutes ago.
7. Light is fast. The universe is bigger.
