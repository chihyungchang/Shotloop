# 04-journey · A light pulse crosses 1 AU in 8 min 19 s

## Must show

The division 149.6 million km ÷ 299,792 km/s ≈ 499 s; a gold pulse from the Sun to Earth; a running mm:ss timer that stops at 08:19; chip "Sunlight crosses 1 AU in about 8 min 19 s".

## Motion

The pulse travels at constant speed (no easing: light does not accelerate) from 0.9 s to 5.0 s while the timer runs 00:00 → 08:19; Earth flashes on arrival, the timer turns gold.
