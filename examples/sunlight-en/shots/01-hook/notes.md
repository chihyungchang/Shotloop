# 01-hook · The Sun, and the question

## Must show

A glowing Sun in deep space with a horizontal lens streak; the question "How long does sunlight take to reach Earth?" as a big headline, "how long" in gold; the line "The Sun you see right now is the Sun as it was" below the Sun. The middle frame is the gallery poster.

## Voice-over

You have never seen the Sun as it is right now. Its light takes time to get here.

## Motion

Slow push in on the Sun; stars drift; kicker, headline and line rise in one after another.
