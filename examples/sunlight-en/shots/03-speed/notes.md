# 03-speed · Speed of light: 299,792 km/s

## Must show

Golden streaks racing across space; a small Earth with a light point lapping its equator; 299,792 km / s; "About 7.5 laps of Earth's equator in 1 second".

## Voice-over

Light in a vacuum covers about 299,792 kilometers every second. That is about 7.5 laps around the equator.

## Motion

Streaks fly left to right at constant speed; the light point circles (slowed so it reads); the number counts up.
