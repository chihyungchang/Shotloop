# Take a phone apart: what’s inside?

## Idea

A 24-second product-film teardown of a generic smartphone (no brand, no
model): a hero shot, an exploded view of its layers, then one close-up
per major part, and the layers closing back up. Everything is built from
Blender primitives and procedural materials.

## Facts

- Most phones are under 1 cm thick (folded foldables are thicker). One
  current example, quoted: "Depth: 0.34 inch (8.75 mm)" (source: Apple,
  "iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max - Technical Specifications",
  https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/, read 31 October 2026)
- OLED: each pixel lights itself, so the panel needs no backlight.
  Quoted: "pixels made up of organic materials that emit light themselves
  when electrical current is applied"; "OLED displays are different from
  LCDs in that it does not require a backlight unit to source light"
  (source: Samsung Display, "[Learn Display] 14. OLED",
  https://global.samsungdisplay.com/28033/)
- The processor, memory and storage chips sit on the one main board.
  Quoted: "On the logic board, we have the A18 Pro System on Chip located
  in the center of the sandwich."; "This SoC contains a 6-core CPU, 6-core
  GPU, and a 16-core neural engine, layered underneath 8GB of LPDDR5
  SDRAM."; "On the reverse side, we have the Kyoxia 128GB NAND flash
  storage" (source: iFixit, "More Modular Than Ever Before: iPhone 16 Pro
  and Pro Max Teardown", 26 September 2024,
  https://www.ifixit.com/News/100693/more-modular-than-ever-before-iphone-16-pro-and-pro-max-teardown)
- The battery takes up a large share of the body. Qualitative only, no
  percentage: it varies by model. Quoted, about one very thin phone: "The
  middle of this phone is basically just a battery with a frame around
  it." (source: iFixit, "Apple's Thinnest iPhone Still Stands Up to Repairs",
  iPhone Air teardown, 20 September 2025,
  https://www.ifixit.com/News/113171/iphone-air-teardown)
- Each rear camera has its own lens and sensor. Quoted: "Each camera has a
  lens for directing light and a sensor for capturing it, both of which
  affect the quality of the finished shot." Not every zoom step is a
  separate camera: some are crops of the main sensor, so the film doesn't
  say so. (source: Popular Science, David Nield, "The extra lenses in your
  smartphone's camera, explained", 28 March 2019,
  https://www.popsci.com/extra-lenses-in-your-smartphones-camera-explained/)
- The layer list in the exploded view (cover glass, display, frame,
  mainboard, battery, camera module, back) is a simplified, generic model
  of the parts named above, not any one phone.
