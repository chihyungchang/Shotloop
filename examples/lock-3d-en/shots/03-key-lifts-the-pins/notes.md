# 03-key-lifts-the-pins · Key lifts the pins

## Must show

Square-on cutaway. The key slides in from the left, its ridged edge riding under the copper key pins; each stack bobs up and down over the bumps and settles at a different height. Tracking with foreground: the key's bow passes close to camera, out of focus, as the camera rides alongside.

## Voice-over

Slide the key in, and each ridge lifts its pin stack to a different height.

(no text)

## Motion

Key travels left to right with ease-in and ease-out; pin heights follow the key profile under each pin (driven by the geometry, not by eye), the springs squeezing and stretching; overshoot as the key seats. Camera tracks right with the key, then holds.

## Sound

quick ticks as pins ride over the ridges; a pop as the key seats
