# 01-hook-the-lock · Hook: the lock

## Must show

Hero image: the lock cutaway, big and lit square on in a dark room, a warm rim light along the brass plug; the key's tip hovers just left of the keyway, pointing right. Housing shown as a clear ghost so the five pin stacks already read inside. Headline in the top band.

## Voice-over

Why does only one key turn this lock?

## On screen

One key. Why?

## Motion

Slow push-in of a few centimetres onto the lock; the key eases a little toward the keyway (anticipation) and stops; hold on the end frame.

## Sound

a soft tick as the key stops at the keyway
