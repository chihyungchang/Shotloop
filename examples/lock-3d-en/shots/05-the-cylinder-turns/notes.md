# 05-the-cylinder-turns · The cylinder turns

## Must show

Camera crane round to a three-quarter view: the brass plug and key turn together, the copper pins turning with the plug while the blue pins stay up in the housing. The cam on the plug's back sweeps the bolt; the bolt slides back out of the strike plate into the lock.

## Voice-over

Nothing blocks it now, so the cylinder turns and the bolt slides back.

## On screen

Unlocked

## Motion

Anticipation: a slight back-turn of the key, then the plug rotates 90° with ease and the cam pushes the bolt back right to left; overlapping settle of the bolt. Camera arcs 40° to see the plug's turn, held.

## Sound

a click as the plug starts to turn; a thud as the bolt stops, after the line
