# 04-splits-line-up · Splits line up

## Must show

Push-in on the cyan shear line: all five splits between blue and copper pins now sit exactly on it. Each split flashes as it lands, left to right, drawing a continuous bright line across the plug's top. Brief inset beat: a wrong key leaves one copper pin poking above the line, red, then the right key returns.

## Voice-over

With the right key, every split between pins lines up exactly at the gap.

## On screen

Lined up

## Motion

Slow push-in on realisation from the whole cutaway to the five splits; the cyan line draws left to right as the splits light; hold at the end.

## Sound

five quiet ticks in a row as the splits light; a ding when the line completes
