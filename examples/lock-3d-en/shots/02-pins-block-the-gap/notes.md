# 02-pins-block-the-gap · Pins block the gap

## Must show

X-ray ghost: the steel housing fades to a faint wireframe so the cutaway is clear, seen square on. Five springs press five stacks down: blue driver pins on top, copper key pins below. The driver pins cross the glowing cyan gap between plug and housing. The plug tries to turn a few degrees, jams against the blue pins and springs back. Label 'shear line' pinned to the cyan gap, off the subject to the left.

## Voice-over

Inside, springs push stacks of pins down across the gap, so the inner cylinder can't turn.

## On screen

Shear line

## Motion

Springs compress and push the stacks down one after another, left to right, as 'springs push' is spoken; on 'can't turn' the plug rattles twice and stops. Camera slow 20° hero orbit from three-quarter view to square on, held at the end.

## Sound

a click as each stack drops (two or three, quiet); a thud as the plug jams, in the pause after the line
