# 03-scattering · 空气分子更爱散射蓝光

## Must show

Two lanes, one N₂ molecule each. Blue 450 nm: strong rings and arrows in every direction, a weak wave carries on (散射 强). Red 700 nm: faint dashed rings, the wave carries on almost whole (散射 弱).

## Motion

Both waves travel left to right; rings spread from each molecule once its wave arrives.
