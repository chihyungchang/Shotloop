# 02-white-light · 阳光是所有颜色的混合

## Must show

A sun on the left; a white beam with all seven colours packed inside it hits a glass prism and fans out into the spectrum, red on top (700 nm), violet at the bottom (400 nm). Caption 一束白光 = 七种颜色.

## Motion

Beam draws to the prism, lights up inside the glass, then the fan opens to the right; a highlight sweeps across the glass.
