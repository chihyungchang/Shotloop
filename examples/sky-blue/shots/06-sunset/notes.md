# 06-sunset · 日落：光穿过更厚的空气

## Must show

The curved Earth with a (much exaggerated) shell of air, a person on top. The sun sinks from overhead to the horizon; the beam to the person gets longer and turns orange while blue dots peel off it. Counter 光穿过的空气 ×1.0 → ×38 (Kasten–Young air mass), chip 正午 / 下午 / 日落. Accent and sky turn sunset orange.

## Motion

Earth rises in, sun glides down along its arc, sky and ground warm, blue dots drift off the beam.
