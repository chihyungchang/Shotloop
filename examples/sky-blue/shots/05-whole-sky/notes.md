# 05-whole-sky · 蓝光从四面八方来

## Must show

A dome of air over the ground, a person (你) under it, the sun at the left with sunlight crossing the dome. Points across the dome light up and send blue lines down to the person, from every direction.

## Motion

Dome outline draws, sun rays sweep in, points light up one by one in a shuffled order, each blue line travels to the person; the dome fills with blue.
