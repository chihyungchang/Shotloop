# 为什么天空是蓝色的？

## Idea

为什么天空是蓝色的？一支 28 秒的竖屏物理小讲解：阳光是所有颜色的混合；
空气分子远小于光的波长，散射强度与波长的四次方成反比，所以蓝光被散射得
最多，从整片天空的各个方向进入眼睛；日落时阳光要穿过厚得多的空气，蓝光
沿途被散射掉，剩下橙和红。

Audience: anyone who has looked up. No equations beyond 1/λ⁴.

## Facts

| Claim in the video | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Sunlight (white light) is a mix of all visible colours; a prism separates them | NASA Space Place, "Why is the sky blue?" |
| Visible light runs from about 400 nm (violet) to 700 nm (red) | Wikipedia, "Visible spectrum" |
| Air molecules (N₂, O₂, ~0.3 nm) are far smaller than the wavelength of light (~450 nm) | Wikipedia, "Rayleigh scattering" (applies to particles much smaller than the wavelength) |
| Scattered intensity ∝ 1/λ⁴ | Wikipedia, "Rayleigh scattering" |
| Blue 450 nm vs red 700 nm: (700/450)⁴ ≈ 5.86, shown as 5.9× | computed from the law above |
| Blue light is scattered all over the sky, so it reaches us from every direction | NASA Space Place, "Why is the sky blue?" |
| At sunset light crosses much more air; relative air mass ≈ 1 overhead, ≈ 38 at the horizon | Kasten & Young (1989), via Wikipedia, "Air mass (astronomy)" |
| Along the long path most blue is scattered out, leaving orange and red | NASA Space Place; Wikipedia, "Rayleigh scattering" |

Simplifications: the sky is not violet mostly because sunlight has less
violet and our eyes are less sensitive to it (Wikipedia); the video
leaves this out. The atmosphere in shots 05–06 is drawn far thicker
than it is.
