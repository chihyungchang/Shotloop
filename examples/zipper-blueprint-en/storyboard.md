# How does a zipper work · Storyboard

| # | Time | Shot | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 s | Hook: the zipper: A tall vertical zipper fills the frame on the blueprint grid: the two fabric tapes and two rows of teeth (left row blue, right row amber) draw on stroke by stroke from top to bottom, open in a V. The slider draws on at the bottom and rides up, closing the teeth behind it into one interlocked column. A callout line snaps out to the slider: 'slider'; another to the teeth: 'teeth'. | Line art traces on; slider travels up with an ease, teeth clicking shut in sequence behind it; the camera eases in slightly. |
| 2 | 5 s | One tooth, exploded: Close-up of the zipper column; one blue tooth lifts out and slides into an exploded view at full size in the centre, rotating to a 3/4 section view and cut open. Callouts draw on: 'bump' pointing to the raised top, 'hollow' to the pocket underneath, with a dimension line across its width. A ghost of the next tooth slides under it to show the bump will sit in that hollow. | Tooth lifts and slides apart from the row, section hatching fills in, callout lines extend and labels type on, the ghost tooth glides into place. |
| 3 | 4.5 s | Two rows, offset: Top view of the two rows of teeth on their tapes, left row blue, right row amber, facing each other across a gap. A dimension marker measures one tooth pitch, then a half-pitch marker shows the right row shifted down by half a tooth. Dashed guide lines from each blue tooth point into a gap on the amber side; the rows slide together and alternate, tooth by tooth. | Dimension lines draw on, the amber row shifts half a pitch, dashed guides flash, then both rows slide inward and interleave. |
| 4 | 5.5 s | Inside the slider: The slider is cut open into a Y-shaped channel, drawn large and centred: two upper arms, one lower throat, labelled 'Y-channel' and 'divider'. Blue teeth stream in through the left arm, amber through the right; as the arms bring them together in the throat each tooth turns flat (an arc marks the turn), its bump nests in the next tooth's hollow, and the meshed column exits at the bottom. Each tooth pulses teal as it locks. | Teeth flow continuously through the channel, angle arcs open and close at the meeting point, teal lock pulses run down the exiting column. |
| 5 | 5 s | Why it holds: The closed zipper column, side by side with a cross-section strip of locked teeth, each bump nested in the next tooth's hollow. Red force arrows pull the left and right tapes outward and run along the row while each bump-in-hollow lock glows teal and does not move. A 'pull' label with growing arrow lengths shows the force rising; the column stays shut. | Red arrows grow outward and tremble, the force fans out tooth by tooth down the column, teal locks pulse steadily, the teeth stay put. |
| 6 | 5 s | Slide it back to open: The same cut-open slider Y-channel as shot 4, now moving down the closed column: the same slider, moving the other way. The divider between the arms (outlined amber-white) parts the meshed teeth; each tooth turns along the same arc and unnests, blue going out the left arm, amber the right. A counter-arrow and a callout read 'one tooth at a time'. | Slider glides down, teeth unhook in sequence with a small tilt and spring apart into the arms, teal lock glows switching off as each tooth leaves. |
| 7 | 3 s | End card: The full zipper from shot 1 zips shut one last time, its slider reaching the top. Beside it a patent-style title block draws on, like a drawing's corner stamp: 'SEPARABLE FASTENER', with the year rolling up to 1917. | Slider races up, title-block border traces on, year counter rolls and lands with a bounce. |
