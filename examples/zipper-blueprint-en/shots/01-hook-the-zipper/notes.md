# 01-hook-the-zipper · Hook: the zipper

## Must show

A tall vertical zipper fills the frame on the blueprint grid: the two fabric tapes and two rows of teeth (left row blue, right row amber) draw on stroke by stroke from top to bottom, open in a V. The slider draws on at the bottom and rides up, closing the teeth behind it into one interlocked column. A callout line snaps out to the slider: 'slider'; another to the teeth: 'teeth'.

## On screen

Tiny teeth, no glue: how does it hold?

## Motion

Line art traces on; slider travels up with an ease, teeth clicking shut in sequence behind it; the camera eases in slightly.
