# 04-inside-the-slider · Inside the slider

## Must show

The slider is cut open into a Y-shaped channel, drawn large and centred: two upper arms, one lower throat, labelled 'Y-channel' and 'wedge'. Blue teeth stream in through the left arm, amber through the right; as they meet in the throat each tooth tilts on an angle marker (an arc showing the tilt), its bump drops into the next tooth's hollow, and the meshed column exits at the bottom. Each tooth pulses teal as it locks.

## On screen

The slider tilts each tooth into the next

## Motion

Teeth flow continuously through the channel, angle arcs open and close at the meeting point, teal lock pulses run down the exiting column.
