# 02-one-tooth-exploded · One tooth, exploded

## Must show

Close-up of the zipper column; one blue tooth lifts out and slides into an exploded view at full size in the centre, rotating to a 3/4 section view and cut open. Callouts draw on: 'bump' pointing to the raised top, 'hollow' to the pocket underneath, with a dimension line across its width. A ghost of the next tooth slides under it to show the bump will sit in that hollow.

## On screen

Every tooth: bump above, hollow below

## Motion

Tooth lifts and slides apart from the row, section hatching fills in, callout lines extend and labels type on, the ghost tooth glides into place.
