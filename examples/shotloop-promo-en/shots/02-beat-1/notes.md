# 02-beat-1 · 01 — The problem

## Must show

Chapter device: mono label "01 — THE PROBLEM" over a hairline rule, SHOTLOOP small at the right; this label style, place and colour hold for every chapter of the film. Under the headline, the row device (screens.js row): seven big shot tiles in one row across the frame, each a wireframe of a film shot with a chip under it; tile 4 is marked wrong with a thin ink outline and a small mono chip "TOO STILL". Then every one of the seven tiles dims and runs a progress sweep — all seven render again — with a small mono label under the row carrying the words the caption no longer says: "whole film re-renders · ~8 min draft". The counter device sits in the footer over a rule: "7 / 7" in red with the small label "shots render again". The row is the only picture, keeps its own proportions and fills the frame's middle band.

## On screen

One shot wrong
All 7 render

## Motion

Headline rises line by line (second line muted), 80 ms apart; the seven tiles rise in a wave left to right, 60 ms apart; the outline snaps onto tile 4; then the sweep runs across all seven in turn and the counter rolls 0 → 7 and turns red. The beat ends on an ink wipe panel starting to cross the frame.

## Sound

a low tick per tile as the row lands, a dull thud as the wrong shot is marked, a rising whirr under the seven sweeps

## Source line

Shotloop, docs/PRD.md: feedback was nearly always about one shot, but each check rendered the whole film (~8 min draft, ~1 h final)
