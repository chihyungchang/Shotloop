# 01-hook · Title card

## Must show

Title card (screens.js titleCard()) on the paper ground, standing alone with no chapter label and no headline beside it: the real logo repo/logo.png at the left, as tall as the capitals of the name, and just after it SHOTLOOP in the site's Archivo black; under them, on the same left edge, a hairline ink rule and, small and secondary in mono, the product line "Short videos with AI — macOS". The lockup sits centred in the frame and fills most of its width; a faint lime field (#d4ff3a) breathes behind the logo only.

## On screen

SHOTLOOP

## Motion

Logo and name rise together out of their mask in a 0.45 s ease-out; the rule draws left to right over 0.4 s, staggered 80 ms; the small mono line fades up last. The whole lockup drifts up about 1% over the beat so nothing is frozen. No bounce.

## Sound

a soft low swell as the lockup lands, one quiet click as the rule finishes drawing
