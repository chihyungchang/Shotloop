# 01-hook-four-words · Hook: four words

## Must show

Four-word stack: KETCHUP, TYCOON, TYPHOON, KOWTOW slam in one by one, huge, white, filling the frame top to bottom. A big '4' in fire orange rolls up from 1 to 4 beside the stack as each word lands, then an orange marker sweeps across the whole stack.

## On screen

4 English words shaped by Chinese

## Motion

Each word drops in from above with a short squash bounce (one every 0.5 s), the counter ticks with them, the marker sweep finishes at 3 s and the stack shakes lightly.
