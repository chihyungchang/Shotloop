# The $1.5B Bybit heist

## Idea

On 21 February 2025, about $1.5 billion in crypto was stolen from the exchange Bybit, the biggest crypto theft on record. The video follows the money: out of a cold wallet, split across thousands of addresses, swapped into bitcoin, and the FBI naming North Korea five days later.

## Hook

About $1.5 billion in crypto was taken from the exchange Bybit in a single hack on 21 February 2025.

## Copy

Roughly 401,000 ETH left one of Bybit's cold wallets, the offline storage exchanges treat as the safest place they have.

No crypto theft before it came close: the previous records, Ronin Network and Poly Network, were around $600 million each.

The thieves moved fast. The funds were split across thousands of addresses on several blockchains and much of it was swapped into bitcoin, which makes it harder to follow.

Five days later, the FBI said North Korea was responsible, naming the group it calls TraderTraitor.

## Facts

- “North Korea Responsible for $1.5 Billion Bybit Hack”; “the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (North Korea) was responsible for the theft of approximately $1.5 billion in virtual assets from cryptocurrency exchange Bybit on or about February 21, 2025.” (source: FBI Public Service Announcement I-022625-PSA, 26 Feb 2025 — https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA250226)
- “TraderTraitor actors are proceeding rapidly and have converted some of the stolen assets to Bitcoin and other virtual assets dispersed across thousands of addresses on multiple blockchains.” (source: FBI Public Service Announcement I-022625-PSA, 26 Feb 2025 — https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA250226)
- About 401,000 ETH (plus smaller amounts of staked ETH tokens), worth about $1.46 billion, was taken from a Bybit Ethereum cold wallet; the largest crypto theft on record. (source: Elliptic, 'Bybit hack largest in history', 21 Feb 2025 — https://www.elliptic.co/blog/bybit-hack-largest-in-history)
- Earlier record thefts: Ronin Network, about $625 million (March 2022); Poly Network, about $611 million (August 2021), values as reported at the time. (source: Elliptic, 'Bybit hack largest in history', 21 Feb 2025 — https://www.elliptic.co/blog/bybit-hack-largest-in-history)
