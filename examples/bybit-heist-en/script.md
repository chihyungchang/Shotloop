# The $1.5B Bybit heist · Script

1. Breaking: 1.5 billion dollars in crypto, gone in a single hack.
2. One transfer emptied Bybit's cold wallet. About 401,000 ether, on the move.
3. That makes it the biggest crypto theft ever, more than twice the old record.
4. The thieves split it across thousands of addresses, and swapped some of it into bitcoin.
5. Five days later, the FBI said who was behind it: North Korea.
6. From hack to attribution: five days.
