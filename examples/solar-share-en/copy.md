# How much of our power is solar?

## Idea

Solar still makes only a small slice of the world's electricity, but it is growing more than any other source: up 30% in 2025, and it alone met three quarters of the rise in demand.

## Hook

How much of the world's electricity comes from the Sun?

## Copy

In 2025, solar made 8.7% of all the electricity the world generated: about 1 unit in every 11.

That is still small. Coal alone made a third, and gas more than a fifth.

But solar is the growth story. Its output rose 30% in one year, a record 636 TWh, to 2,778 TWh. Solar alone met 75% of the rise in the world's electricity demand.

For the fourth year running, solar grew more than any other source. Small slice, biggest growth.

## Facts

- Solar generated 8.7% of global electricity in 2025, almost level with nuclear. Quote: "Solar reached 8.7% of global generation in 2025, almost matching nuclear's share (8.9%)". (About 1 in 11 is our arithmetic: 100 ÷ 8.7 ≈ 11.5.) (source: Ember, World electricity data page (Global Electricity Review 2026 data) — https://ember-energy.org/countries-and-regions/world/)
- Shares of global electricity in 2025: coal 33% ("Coal fell to 33%, dropping below one-third for the first time"), gas 21.8%, hydro 14% ("Hydro remains the largest individual source of low-carbon electricity at 14%"), nuclear 8.9%, wind 8.5% ("wind rose to 8.5%"), solar 8.7%. 'Other' (5.1%) is the remainder, our arithmetic. (source: Ember, World electricity data page (Global Electricity Review 2026 data) — https://ember-energy.org/countries-and-regions/world/)
- Solar generation grew 30% in 2025, adding a record 636 TWh. Quote: "Solar power increased by a record 636 TWh to reach 2,778 TWh in 2025, a 30% increase". (2024 ≈ 2,778 − 636 = 2,142 TWh is our arithmetic.) (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2026, published 21 April 2026 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2026/)
- Solar met three quarters of the growth in electricity demand in 2025. Quote: "Solar power alone met 75% of the net increase in electricity demand". (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2026, published 21 April 2026 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2026/)
- Solar had the largest absolute growth of any source for the fourth year in a row. Quote: "For the fourth year running, solar recorded the largest absolute growth of any electricity source." (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2026, published 21 April 2026 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2026/)
