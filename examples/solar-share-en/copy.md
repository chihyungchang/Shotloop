# How much of the world's electricity comes from solar power t

## Idea

Solar still makes only a small slice of the world's electricity, but it is growing faster than any other source: up 29% in a single year and doubled in three.

## Hook

How much of the world's electricity comes from the Sun?

## Copy

In 2024, solar made 6.9% of all the electricity the world generated: about 1 unit in every 15.

That is still small. Coal alone made about a third, and gas more than a fifth.

But solar is the speed story. Its output grew 29% in one year, adding 474 TWh, and it has doubled in just three years since 2021.

For the third year in a row, solar was the world's largest source of new electricity. Small slice, fastest growth.

## Facts

- Solar generated 6.9% of global electricity in 2024 (2,131 TWh). (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2025 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2025/)
- Shares of global electricity in 2024: coal ≈34%, gas ≈22%, hydro ≈14%, nuclear ≈9%, wind ≈8%, solar ≈7%. (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2025 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2025/)
- Solar generation grew 29% in 2024, adding 474 TWh. (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2025 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2025/)
- Global solar generation doubled in the three years since 2021. (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2025 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2025/)
- Solar was the largest source of new electricity generation worldwide for the third year in a row in 2024. (source: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2025 — https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2025/)
