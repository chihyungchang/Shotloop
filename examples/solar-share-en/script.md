# How much of our power is solar? · Script

1. How much of our electricity comes from the Sun? In 2025, about 1 unit in every 11.
2. That is still small. Coal alone made a third of all power, and gas more than a fifth.
3. But solar is the growth story. Its output rose 30% in one year, a record 636 terawatt hours.
4. Solar alone met three quarters of the rise in world electricity demand.
5. For the fourth year running, solar grew more than any other source.
6. A small slice of world power, with the biggest growth.
