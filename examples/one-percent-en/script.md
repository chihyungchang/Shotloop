# 1% better every day: what's a year worth? · Script

Voice-over (one spoken line per shot, subtitled automatically); the on-screen text is the numbers and short labels.

1. 1% a day sounds tiny. But what happens if you keep it up, or let it slip, for a whole year?
2. Multiply by 1.01 every day. You have doubled by day 70, and after a year you are at about 37.78.
3. Now take 0.99 every day. Half is gone by day 69, and a year later only about 2.6% is left.
4. On a log scale, both become straight lines. After a year, one is about 1,481 times the other.
5. Of course, nobody improves by exactly 1% a day. What really compounds is a small habit, kept up day after day.
6. So don't wait for one big day. Show up every day.
