# 02-up · 1.01 to the 365th: the curve climbs to 37.78

## Must show

- Linear chart, x 0–365 days, y 0–40, hairline grid and mono tick labels.
- Green curve of 1.01^day with a soft fill; a tip dot labelled with the day.
- Counter 1.01^day, ending at 37.78 (1.01^365 = 37.783).
- Day-70 marker: "Day 70 · doubled" (1.01^70 = 2.007).

## Voice-over

Multiply by 1.01 every day. You have doubled by day 70, and after a year you are at about 37.78.
