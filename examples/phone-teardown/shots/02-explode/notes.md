# 02-explode · The phone comes apart in layers

## Must show

- Seven layers separating into a rising diagonal stack: cover glass (black ink border), display, frame, mainboard, battery, camera module, back glass.
- 「爆炸图 / 一层一层叠起来 / 整台手机，厚度通常不到一厘米」 in the top third, clear of the stack.

## Motion

Explode 0.35–3.9 s (eased) while the phone squares up and the camera pulls back, rises and swings left.
