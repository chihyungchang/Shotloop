# 05-battery-camera · 电池与摄像头模组

## Must show

- First half: the battery, the largest part, out of the stack with its flex cable and connector; 「03 · 电池 / 占机身体积的很大一部分」.
- Second half: the camera module turned round, three lenses and the flash facing us; 「04 · 摄像头 / 摄像头模组 / 几颗镜头，各管不同的焦段」.

## Motion

Board returns home (0–0.9 s), battery slides out left (0–1.5 s) and back (2.4–3.3 s); camera module comes forward to the right turning 162° (2.4–3.9 s). The camera follows the battery, then swings across to the module (2.3–3.8 s).
