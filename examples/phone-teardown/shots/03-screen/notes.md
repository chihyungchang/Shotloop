# 03-screen · 屏幕（OLED）

## Must show

- The display panel, out of the stack and facing the camera; the frame, board and battery behind it.
- 「01 · 屏幕 / 屏幕（OLED） / 每个像素自己发光，不需要背光」.

## Motion

Cover glass slides away left, the display comes forward and turns 12°. The screen switches off top-down (0.55–0.95 s), then lights row by row from the bottom with a bright scan line (1.15–2.7 s): self-lit pixels, no backlight. Slow push in.
