# 拆开一部手机：里面有什么？

## Idea

A 24-second product-film teardown of a generic smartphone (no brand, no
model): a hero shot, an exploded view of its layers, then one close-up
per major part, and the layers closing back up. Everything is built from
Blender primitives and procedural materials.

## Facts

- A modern smartphone is usually under 1 cm thick, yet stacks cover
  glass, display, frame, mainboard, battery, camera module and back.
- OLED: each pixel emits its own light, so the panel needs no backlight
  (unlike LCD).
- The mainboard carries the processor (SoC), memory and storage chips,
  soldered on.
- The battery takes up a large share of the body's volume (no figure
  given: it varies by model).
- Phones with several rear cameras give each lens a different focal
  length (wide, ultra-wide, telephoto), each with its own image sensor.

## Sources
