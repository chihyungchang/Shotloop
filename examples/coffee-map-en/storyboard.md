Then the growing countries light leaf green one by one from the Americas to Africa to Asia, while a small counter beside the band ticks up to 70+. | Globe-to-flat morph; tropic lines draw left to right; band fades in; country fills ripple west to east; counter ticks to 70+. |
| 3 | 5 s | Brazil leads: Camera flies from the world into South America. Brazil fills a brighter leaf green and grows a gold pin with rank badge '1'. Beside it a big counter rolls to ≈1/3, and a split bar at the bottom (the world's coffee) splits: Brazil's third in green, the rest in dim slate. | Zoom and pan into Brazil; the fill pulses brighter; pin drops; counter rolls; split bar wipes and separates into its two shares. |
| 4 | 5 s | The top five: Camera pulls back out to the coffee belt. Rank pins drop in turn with their badges: 2 Vietnam (labelled 'robusta'), 3 Colombia, 4 Indonesia, 5 Ethiopia, each country brightening to the same leaf green as Brazil, which keeps its '1' pin. | Camera glides east across the belt; each pin drops with a bounce as the camera passes; country names type on beside the pins. |
| 5 | 4.5 s | Where arabica began: Camera flies into East Africa. Ethiopia's highlands glow warm gold inside its green fill; its '5' pin stays, and a small coffee-plant icon sprouts on the highlands with the label 'arabica'. Faint ripples spread from it across the map. | Zoom into the Horn of Africa; gold glow breathes; plant icon grows leaf by leaf; ripple rings expand outward. |
| 6 | 6 s | Routes north: Camera pulls out to the full flat map with the belt and the five green producers. Coffee-orange arcs draw on from Brazil, Colombia, Vietnam, Indonesia and Ethiopia to two destinations that light cool blue: the European Union and the United States. Small bean dots run along every arc like a flow. | Arcs draw on one after another; destination fills fade in blue; bean dots stream continuously along the arcs; camera drifts slowly north. |
| 7 | 3 s | End card: The globe again, slowly turning, wrapped in the glowing orange routes from the previous shot, bean dots still flowing; the title 'Where does the world's coffee come from' settles above it. | Globe turns; arcs shimmer; bean dots keep running; title fades and settles. |
