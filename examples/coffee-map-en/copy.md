# Where does the world's coffee come from

## Idea

Where the world's coffee comes from: the band of tropical countries that grow it, the few that dominate, and the routes that carry it to the cups of Europe and North America.

## Hook

About 184 million bags of coffee are grown in a year. Where?

## Copy

About 184 million 60-kilo bags of coffee were produced in 2025/26.

It grows in more than 40 countries, mostly in a band around the equator, between the tropics: the coffee belt.

One country leads by far: Brazil grows nearly 40% of the world's coffee.

Next come Vietnam, mostly robusta, then Colombia, Ethiopia and Indonesia.

Coffee's story begins in Ethiopia, where ancient coffee forests still grow.

From the belt, the beans sail north: the European Union and the United States are the biggest importers.

Every cup, a long journey.

## Facts

- World coffee production is estimated at about 184 million 60-kg bags in coffee year 2025/26 (183.6 million, up from 175.9 million in 2024/25, ICO Table 3). Quote: "World coffee production is estimated to increase by 4.4% to 183.6 million bags in the current coffee year 2025/26". (source: International Coffee Organization, Coffee Market Report, August 2026, https://www.ico.org/documents/cy2025-26/cmr-0826-e.pdf)
- Coffee is grown in more than 40 countries, mostly between the Tropics of Cancer and Capricorn (the 'coffee belt'). Quote: "In more than 40 countries, mostly in a narrow climactic zone along the Equator between the Tropics of Cancer and Capricorn (25°N to 30°S)!" (source: National Coffee Association USA, 'Coffee Regions of the World', aboutcoffee.org, modified 31 July 2026, https://aboutcoffee.org/origins/coffee-regions-of-the-world/)
- Brazil is the largest producer, with nearly 40% of world output. Quote: "Brazil accounts for nearly 40 percent of world production". (source: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service, Coffee: World Markets and Trade, July 2026, https://apps.fas.usda.gov/psdonline/circulars/coffee.pdf)
- The top five producers (2026/27 forecast) are, in order, Brazil, Vietnam, Colombia, Ethiopia ("a record 12.1 million" bags) and Indonesia (11.4 million); Vietnam grows mostly robusta (Robusta 31,400 of 32,500 thousand bags). (source: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service, Coffee: World Markets and Trade, July 2026, https://apps.fas.usda.gov/psdonline/circulars/coffee.pdf)
- Coffee was first discovered in Ethiopia. Quote: "the benefits of coffee beans and brewed coffee were first discovered on the Ethiopian Plateau, where ancient coffee forests still thrive today." (source: National Coffee Association USA, aboutcoffee.org, modified 31 July 2026, https://aboutcoffee.org/origins/coffee-regions-of-the-world/)
- The European Union and the United States are the largest coffee importers (USDA "Total Imports" table order: European Union, United States, Japan). (source: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service, Coffee: World Markets and Trade, July 2026, https://apps.fas.usda.gov/psdonline/circulars/coffee.pdf)
