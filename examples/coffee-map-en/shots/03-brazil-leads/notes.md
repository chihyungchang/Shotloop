# 03-brazil-leads · Brazil leads

## Must show

Camera flies from the world into South America. Brazil fills a brighter leaf green and grows a gold pin with rank badge '1'. Beside it a big counter rolls to ≈1/3, and a split bar at the bottom (the world's coffee) splits: Brazil's third in green, the rest in dim slate.

## On screen

Brazil grows about 1 in 3 bags

## Motion

Zoom and pan into Brazil; the fill pulses brighter; pin drops; counter rolls; split bar wipes and separates into its two shares.
