# 06-routes-north · Routes north

## Must show

Camera pulls out to the full flat map with the belt and the five green producers. Coffee-orange arcs draw on from Brazil, Colombia, Vietnam, Indonesia and Ethiopia to two destinations that light cool blue: the European Union and the United States. Small bean dots run along every arc like a flow.

## Voice-over

From the belt, the beans sail north. The European Union and the United States are the biggest importers.

## On screen

Most sails north to Europe and the US

## Motion

Arcs draw on one after another; destination fills fade in blue; bean dots stream continuously along the arcs; camera drifts slowly north.
