# Where does the world's coffee come from · Script

1. About 184 million bags of coffee are grown in a year. So where does it all come from?
2. It grows in more than 40 nations, mostly in a band around the equator: the coffee belt.
3. One country leads by far. Brazil grows nearly 40 percent of all the coffee in the world.
4. Next come Vietnam, mostly robusta, then Colombia, Ethiopia and Indonesia.
5. The story of coffee begins in Ethiopia, where ancient coffee forest still grows.
6. From the belt, the beans sail north. The European Union and the United States are the biggest importers.
7. Every cup, a long journey.
